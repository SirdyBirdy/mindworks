In the room

What does a session with Alina actually feel like?

"A session with me might feel like listening to your own voice, but hearing it differently."

Sessions can be fun, giggly, and energetic just as easily as they can be calm and grounding — it depends on the day and what you're carrying into the room. One client described leaving a session feeling like they'd "just come from servicing." Alina takes that as a compliment.

Her story

How did she get into this work?

At 15, Alina attended a suicide prevention workshop run by Connecting. It taught her how much difference listening can make, and what it actually means to not judge someone. She's tried other things since, but she keeps coming back to this — and at this point, she's fairly certain it's what she was made for.

Outside sessions, she's drawn to why the same circumstances play out so differently for different people — what actually shifts things, what doesn't, and why. Small moments from the therapy room stay with her too: a stifled laugh, someone tearing up mid-sentence, a joke that lands. Stories tend to linger.

Before you start

What she wishes people knew before starting therapy

"Therapy isn't a quick process. Change won't necessarily be a 'Eureka!' moment — it's often subtle shifts in how you think."

Alina also wants feedback, not silence — tell her what's working and what isn't, and she'll try to shape her approach around that. If it still doesn't click, that's okay too.

FAQ

Common questions

I'm not sure I'm "queer enough" to see a queer-affirmative therapist.
Queer-affirmative just means I don't assume heterosexuality or cisgender identity as a default, and I won't treat your identity as something to be fixed. Anyone questioning, exploring, or existing outside the default is welcome here.
Do I have to talk about my trauma?
No. We go at your pace, always. Trauma therapy doesn't mean reliving everything — it means understanding how the past shows up in the present, and doing that carefully and only when you're ready.
What's the discovery call for?
It's a free 15-minute conversation — specifically for therapy enquiries only. We use it to see if we're a good fit before you commit to a full session. No paperwork, no pressure.
