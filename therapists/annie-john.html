Approach

How she works

"I aim for sessions to be warm, comfortable, and collaborative, where you can be honest about what you're experiencing, and leave a little lighter."

Annie works with a trauma-informed and queer-affirmative lens, drawing on CBT and Narrative therapy depending on what's actually needed. The aim isn't to arrive at a session with things figured out. It's to meet you where you're at, and help you feel less alone in what you're carrying.

Modalities she uses: Trauma-informed therapy · Queer-affirmative CBT · Narrative therapy

In the room

What does a session with Annie actually feel like?

Safe and non-judgemental, without the pressure of having things figured out before you walk in. Annie meets people where they are, and sessions tend to feel less like an assessment and more like a conversation, one that's honest, unhurried, and collaborative.

Her story

How did she get into this work?

During her bachelor's degree, Annie volunteered with an NGO running life-skills and personal development sessions in schools. She was paired with a psychologist who worked closely with students who reached out for support, and watching how that psychologist showed up for them through difficult periods and transitions is what drew her toward this work.

Outside of sessions, Annie is still curious about what shapes people and why we do what we do. She reads about history and watches crime documentaries, drawn to the patterns and experiences behind people's choices, and she's often quietly people-watching, wondering what story someone might be carrying.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you only work with people who have a diagnosis?
No. A lot of the people I work with don't have a diagnosis and don't need one. They're going through something hard and want support making sense of it. That's enough of a reason to start.
What's the first session actually like?
We spend most of it understanding what's brought you in. I'll ask questions to get a real sense of you and your life, not to assess you clinically. By the end, we'll have a rough sense of how we might work together.
How long does therapy usually take?
It depends on what you're working through. Therapy is an ongoing process, some people find things shift in a handful of months, others find more value in longer-term work. I'll be honest with you about what I think is needed, and you're always free to stop or pause.
What's the discovery call for?
It's a free 15-minute conversation, only for therapy enquiries. We use it to see if we're a good fit before you commit to a full session. No paperwork, no pressure.
Do you offer in-person sessions?
Yes, I see clients in person at our Pune clinic, as well as online. If you'd like to meet in person, mention that when you book and we'll arrange it.
