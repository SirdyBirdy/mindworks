About us

A team of certified counselling professionals in Pune, working in person and online since 2016.

Whatever you want to talk about, we listen. And hence, Mindworks might just be the place for you. We accept you and help you accept and believe in yourself, fully. We, with the help of our psychologists and psychotherapists, help you reflect on anything that troubles your mind.

At Mindworks we help you with a mental health assessment, counselling and training, judgement-free. Feeling overwhelmed, depressed, anxious, angry or stressed is unexplainable, and so is feeling like you would never get over the trauma or PTSD. Going through issues in your relationship, marriage, family, or even professionally, is difficult, but going through them alone is beyond tough. Therapy can help you understand yourself better and help you build self-confidence, learn to develop healthy boundaries, manage emotions effectively, sleep better, among other things. We aren't trying to fix you — we're trying to shed some light on things that could have slipped your mind.

When it comes to guidance or help, you might have tried to talk to someone — a friend, someone from the family — but none of it has seemed to work out. Going to therapy or seeking professional help might be your last option, and that's okay. We're here to guide you through it all. You don't have to sit around, suffering till you can't take it anymore — you can seek professional help whenever you want. We at Mindworks have a team of certified counselling professionals, who specialise in helping people identify and achieve their personal goals by coping with the issues that are causing them distress, anxiety, and stress. We take great pride in the progress and success of our clients, and look forward to helping you.

We provide online counselling as well as in-person sessions in Pune, over video or phone, so distance or timing doesn't have to be the reason you go without support.
