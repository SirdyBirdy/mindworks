Brand assets
------------
Put your logo files here, then set their paths in content/site.js (site.logo, site.logoOnDark, site.favicon).

  logo.svg / logo.png        logo for the light nav bar (dark artwork)
  logo-light.svg / .png      logo for the dark green footer (light artwork) - optional
  favicon.png                square browser-tab icon, 64x64 or larger

SVG is best (sharp at any size). PNG: export at 2x the display height (nav shows it at ~30px tall, so 60px+ tall).
